Project designed, developed and pitched in 2 weeks during the Apple Foundation Program at RMIT.

DonateMate is an IOS app designed to be used by charities to connect struggling communities to people with unused household items. Rather than listing an item for free on Facebook Marketplace or GumTree where untrustworthy resellers are more likely than struggling individuals, list your item on DonateMate! A reliable member of a charity will pick up your item, deliver it to a person in need and relay their thanks.
